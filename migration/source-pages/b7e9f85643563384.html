Frequently Asked Questions
Is Tangier worth visiting?

Tangier offers a unique amalgam of myriad cultures thanks to the rich history it boasts itself with. Since it was an international zone, you can taste different cultures and lifestyles while visiting the city.

What documents do I need for a private day trip from Spain to Morocco?

You need a valid passport with at least six months’ validity. Depending on your nationality, a visa may be required. Please visit the official website for the most up-to-date information.

How long does the trip from Spain to Morocco take?

The trip duration depends on your departure point in Spain. For example, from Tarifa to Tangier by ferry, it takes around 1 hour. Including border control and travel time, expect the full round trip to last approximately 10-12 hours.

Can I customize my private day trip itinerary?

Yes, all our private day trips are fully customizable. You can choose your preferred departure time, destinations, and activities in Morocco to create a unique experience that fits your interests and schedule.

Are the private day trips suitable for families and children?

Absolutely! Our private day trips are family-friendly, and we can tailor activities and pace to suit travelers of all ages, including children.

Is it safe to travel from Spain to Morocco for a day trip?

Yes, traveling from Spain to Morocco is generally safe. We ensure all transfers and activities comply with safety standards. Our guides are experienced, and we follow the latest travel advisories and health regulations.

How do I book a private day trip from Spain to Morocco?

You can easily book through our website, tangierexcursions.com by selecting your preferred trip, date, and filling out the booking form. Our team will confirm your reservation promptly.

What happens if my ferry is delayed or canceled?

In case of ferry delays or cancellations, we monitor updates closely and will adjust your trip accordingly. We offer flexible rescheduling options to ensure you don’t miss out on your experience.

Do I need to inform you about any dietary restrictions or special needs in advance?

Yes, please inform us at the time of booking if you have any dietary restrictions, mobility issues, or other special requirements so we can accommodate you comfortably.
