Best Private Desert Trip to Morocco From Spain 11 Days / 10 Nights

OVERVIEW

Are you in Spain or Portugal? Planning to include Morocco & Africa in your vacation? The following private Morocco Desert Trip from Spain itinerary is carefully designed by local travel experts to offer you a great insight into the culture & unique lifestyle of Moroccans. Morocco is the closest gateway to Europe from Africa making it easy to step into, cross the Strait of Gibraltar by ferry to Tangier from Tarifa & meet your private driver through his eyes you will explore the hidden aspects of this magical destination.

This Desert Trip to Morocco from Spain is private, purely-cultural & fully customizable. You can expect to cover the mysterious city of Tangier, the artistic city of Asilah, the beautifully blue-hued city of Chefchaouen, the Roman capital of Volubilis, the French Versaille city of Meknes, the spiritual capital of Fes, the majestic Sahara Desert, Atlas Mountains, canyons, Valleys, Kasbahs, Marrakesh and much more.
