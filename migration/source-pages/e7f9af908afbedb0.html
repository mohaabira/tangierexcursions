About Tangier Excursions LLC

Licensed & Experienced Guides

Knowledgeable locals who love sharing Morocco’s true spirit.

Luxury Services

Handpicked accommodations, private transfers, and personalized itineraries.

Authentic Experiences

Go beyond the highlights to experience Morocco like a local.

Quality Over Price

While our tours may cost more, the value, comfort, and exclusivity far exceed expectations.

At Tangier Excursions LLC, we believe travel is more than just sightseeing – it is about connection, discovery, and meaningful experiences. As one of the leading tour operators offering private day trips to Morocco from Spain, our mission is to open the doors to Morocco’s rich culture while ensuring you travel in comfort and style.

Who We Are

We are passionate travel specialists who design tailor-made Morocco tours from Spain. Every journey with us blends luxury services with authentic experiences, so our guests enjoy the perfect balance of comfort and cultural immersion. From the moment you step onto Moroccan soil, our licensed guides are by your side, sharing stories, traditions, and hidden gems that most travelers never get to see.

What Sets Us Apart

Unlike many companies that focus on low-cost, crowded tours, we do not compete on price, we compete on quality. Our approach is simple: fewer compromises, more memorable moments. With us, you won’t just “visit Morocco,” you will live it. 

Whether it’s savoring traditional Moroccan cuisine in a family-owned riad, wandering through vibrant souks with an expert guide, or relaxing in premium transportation, every detail is designed to give you peace of mind.

Through tourism, we found a way to safeguard our traditions and promote cultural preservation. Tangier Excursions became our vessel for connecting travelers with the soul of Morocco. Our family members became expert guides, leading guests through the ancient medinas, across the golden dunes, and into the heart of Moroccan culture.

Our Promise

With Tangier Excursions LLC, you can expect more than just a tour. You can expect an enriching journey that leaves you with unforgettable memories. Whether you are looking for luxury day trips to Morocco from Spain or multi-day luxury Morocco tours from Spain, our team is here to make your travel dreams come true.
